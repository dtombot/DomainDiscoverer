A registrar with the lowest introductory price is not always the cheapest place to keep a domain. Registration lasts for a limited term, and renewals can cost more than the first year.

Rather than publish a quickly outdated price table, here is a comparison method that still works when promotions change.

## What should you compare?

| Item | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| First-year registration | Helps estimate the initial cost |
| Renewal price | Indicates the recurring expense |
| Transfer policy and price | Affects how easily you can change registrar |
| Registration-data privacy | May limit exposure of eligible personal details |
| DNS controls | Needed when connecting hosting, email or other services |
| Auto-renewal settings | Helps avoid an unexpected domain expiry |
| Support and account security | Important if you lose access or need urgent help |

Always compare the same domain extension and registration period across registrars.

## Registrars to include in your shortlist

**Porkbun:** Often considered for straightforward registration and included privacy options where applicable. Check the current pricing and eligibility for your exact TLD.

**Namecheap:** Offers a broad registrar search and sometimes first-year promotions. Read the renewal figure before deciding.

**Cloudflare Registrar:** Has an at-cost pricing approach. Check whether your preferred extension and needed services are supported and review the relevant account requirements.

These examples are not a current price leaderboard or a claim that one company will always be cheaper than another.

## What happened to WHOIS privacy?

Domain registration-data services have evolved. For many generic TLDs, **RDAP** is now the modern registration-data access protocol. Privacy arrangements depend on the registrar, registry, jurisdiction and domain type.

ICANN explains the change in its [RDAP guidance](https://www.icann.org/rdap/). Do not assume every extension offers the same privacy treatment, or that a "free WHOIS privacy" label applies to all names.

## Common hidden-cost traps

- A first-year promotion followed by a higher standard renewal fee.
- Optional products added during checkout.
- Premium domain prices that differ from ordinary registrations.
- Transfer restrictions or conditions you did not review.
- A domain whose eligibility requirements you do not meet.

## Is a registrar different from a host?

Yes. You can use separate providers for registration and web hosting. Our [domain vs hosting guide](/blog/domain-name-vs-web-hosting) explains the distinction.

## A simple way to make your choice

First [check whether your name is available](/domain-availability-checker). Then open two or three registrars directly, compare the first and renewal terms for the same extension, and choose one whose account controls you understand.

Save your invoices, enable supported account security features and check renewal settings after purchase.
